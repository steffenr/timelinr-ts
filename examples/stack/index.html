  • 1900 — A new century

    The city added its first electric streetlights this year, and the old gas lamps went dark for good.

  • 1930 — Hard times

    Storefronts sat empty along the main road as the depression tightened its grip on daily life.

  • 1944 — Turning point

    Supply lines shifted overnight, and for the first time in years the harbor saw new construction.

  • 1950 — Rebuilding

    Cranes returned to the skyline as entire blocks were rebuilt from the ground up.

  • 1971 — First steps

    The transit line opened three stops early, connecting neighborhoods that had never been linked before.

  • 1977 — New wave

    A wave of independent venues opened downtown, reshaping the local music scene almost overnight.

  • 1989 — Walls fall

    Crowds gathered at the border crossing as decades-old barriers came down within hours.

  • 1999 — Millennium eve

    Fireworks lit the harbor as the city counted down to a new millennium amid cautious optimism.

  • 2001 — A new era

    The first fiber lines reached downtown offices, and dial-up modems finally became a memory.

  • 2011 — Connected world

    Free public wifi reached every district, and the last of the payphones were finally removed.
